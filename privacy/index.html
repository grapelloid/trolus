Who we are

Trolus is published by DC network solutions OU (“we”). This policy covers the Trolus app for iPhone and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data. Trolus has no sign-up, contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Where your requests go

When you tap Send, Trolus sends the request directly from your iPhone to the address you entered. The URL, headers, body and any token or password you add go only to that server. What happens to that data there is governed by the owner of that server, not by us.

What stays on your iPhone

To make the app useful, Trolus saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • History: your last 200 requests and their responses, including URLs, headers and bodies, with each response body kept up to 2 MB
  • The request you are editing, including any Bearer token, username or password you typed
  • Environments and settings: your variables and the request timeout

This data never leaves your device through Trolus. Like other app data, it can be included in your iPhone backups to iCloud or your computer, depending on your backup settings. You can delete single requests or all history in the app, and deleting Trolus removes all of its data.

Local network access

If you send a request to an address on your local network, iOS asks for permission to use the local network. Trolus uses it only to reach the servers you address. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

Crash reports from Apple

If you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. We use them only to fix problems. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Children

Trolus is a developer tool and is not directed at children. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Trolus handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub or see Support.
